04

Adopting a winner

  1. Header — swap the plain square in .brand-mark for an <img> of the chosen mark (e.g. brand/cursor-mark.svg) at 28–30px.
  2. Favicon — replace the data-URI icon in all three pages with <link rel="icon" href="brand/<concept>-favicon.svg">.
  3. Social card — drop the chosen mark into the brand row of og-image.svg, then re-render to og-image.png.
